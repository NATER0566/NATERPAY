NATER-PAY Seller Guide

1. Selling on the Global Marketplace

When you publish a product and set it to "Active", it automatically appears on the NATER-PAY public marketplace. Anyone in the world can search for your product, view your cover image, and purchase it instantly using our secure Paystack checkout.

2. Direct Payment Links & QR Codes

Don't want to wait for marketplace traffic? Go to "My Products" and click Copy Link. You can paste this link on WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram. You can also click the QR Code icon to download an image that buyers can scan with their phone camera to pay you in person.

3. Understanding the Central Fee Engine

To process bank and card payments securely, the system applies a processing fee (Paystack Gateway + Platform Commission). You have two choices when creating a product:

Charge Buyer (Recommended): If your product is ₦5,000, the buyer will be charged a little extra (e.g., ₦5,150) to cover the processing fees. You will receive exactly ₦5,000 in your wallet.

Absorb Fee: The buyer pays exactly ₦5,000. The system deducts the processing fees, and you receive the remainder (e.g., ₦4,850) in your wallet.

4. Getting Paid

As soon as a buyer completes a purchase, the Net Revenue is instantly deposited into your NATER-PAY Wallet. You can view your funds on the main Dashboard and request a withdrawal straight to your local bank account at any time.

5. Automated Digital Delivery (Redirect URL)

Selling an E-Book, Software, or private WhatsApp group link? Paste the link in the Redirect URL box when creating your product. The moment a buyer's payment is successful, our system will automatically redirect them to your file or group. You make sales while you sleep.
