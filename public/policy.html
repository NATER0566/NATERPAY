1. Digital Products (VTU, Airtime, Data)

Due to the immediate and automated nature of digital product fulfillment, transactions for Airtime, Data, and Utility bills are generally non-refundable once marked as "Successful" by our providers.

However, if your wallet is debited but the service is not delivered (failed status) due to a network timeout or provider downtime, our Automated Reconciliation system will automatically process a full refund to your NATER-PAY wallet within 24 hours.

2. Fund Withdrawals

If a withdrawal request from your NATER-PAY wallet to your local bank account fails or is rejected by the banking network, the exact withdrawal amount plus the processing fee will be refunded entirely back to your NATER-PAY wallet balance.

3. Account Funding

Deposits made to your NATER-PAY wallet via card or dedicated virtual accounts are final. If you wish to retrieve your funds, you must utilize the standard Withdrawal feature, which is subject to standard processing fees and KYC limits.

4. Dispute Resolution

If you experience an issue requiring manual intervention, please contact our Customer Care team with your Transaction Reference ID. We aim to resolve all escalated transaction disputes within 3 to 5 business days.
